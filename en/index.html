GOOD TO KNOW

Before you start.

Which streaming services does it work with?＋

DualSub is a desktop extension for Prime Video and Netflix. It is not guaranteed to work with every title or subtitle language, so please check it with your own titles on the free version before buying.

Does it work with Disney+?＋

Yes, as a preview in the Chrome version (selected titles). It works on titles where DualSub can confirm supported official subtitles and the playback time, and needs extension version 1.0.12 or later. It is not available in the Edge version. Which subtitles exist depends on the title.

Can I use it on my phone?＋

The DualSub described on this page is for Chrome and Edge on desktop. It is not an extension for mobile streaming apps.

Is the settings screen available in English?＋

Yes. Choose English or Japanese under "Menu language" in the settings. By default it follows your browser's display language when supported. This is separate from the languages you choose for subtitles.

What if the subtitles don't appear?＋

First make sure subtitles are turned on in the streaming service, and reload any video tab that was open before you installed DualSub. If the title doesn't have the subtitles you want, or your browser or language doesn't support on-device translation, they may not appear as expected.

Can it translate languages without official subtitles?＋

On-device translation is available with supported browsers, languages and setups. It is not available everywhere, and translation quality depends on the content.

Are the features different between Free and Pro?＋

No, the features are the same. Free gives you 20 minutes a day, and Pro removes DualSub's daily time limit. Either way, you need your own subscription to the streaming service.
